`spatial_expectation2d`, and `spatial_soft_argmax2d` through it, now weigh a float16 or bfloat16 probability map in float32 and round the coordinate once. The sum used to run in half precision, so the coordinate was not the half-precision rounding of the float32 sum: masses 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.4 at pixels (1, 0), (2, 0), (3, 1) and (7, 3) came out at x = 4.1875 in bfloat16 and y = 1.4990234375 in float16, where the float32 sum rounds to 4.21875 and 1.5.
